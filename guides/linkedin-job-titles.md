# LinkedIn Job Titles: What Recruiters Actually Search For (2025-2026)

> Research date: 2026-03-18

## TL;DR — What to Put in Open to Work (5 slots)

1. **Senior Backend Engineer**
2. **AI Engineer**
3. **Blockchain Engineer**
4. **Senior Software Engineer**
5. **Platform Engineer** (or Distributed Systems Engineer)

---

## 1. "Backend" vs "Back End" vs "Back-End"

**LinkedIn DOES NOT match variants automatically.** This is critically important.

- LinkedIn Recruiter treats `backend`, `back-end`, and `back end` as **three different strings**
- A recruiter searching for "Backend Engineer" will NOT see profiles with "Back-End Engineer" or "Back End Engineer"
- Experienced recruiters use Boolean: `("backend" OR "back-end" OR "back end")`, but many don't bother

**Which format dominates:**
- **"Backend"** (one word, no hyphen) — industry standard since ~2020. Google, Meta, Stripe all use "Backend Engineer"
- **"Back-End"** — becoming outdated, but still seen in enterprise
- **"Back End"** (two words) — rare, mostly non-tech companies

**Recommendation:** Use **"Backend"** everywhere — in headline, experience, Open to Work. This is what most recruiters search for.

## 2. "Blockchain Developer" vs "Blockchain Engineer"

**Both are used, but with different connotations:**

| | Blockchain Developer | Blockchain Engineer |
|---|---|---|
| **Perception** | More general, entry-mid level | More senior, systems-level |
| **Usage** | Standalone title, more common on job boards | Increasingly used in LinkedIn titles |
| **LinkedIn** | Was #1 Emerging Job per LinkedIn | Growing as a senior-level title |
| **Reality** | Titles are not standardized, used interchangeably | In large companies may mean protocol-level work |

**Important nuance:** "Blockchain Engineer" has not yet solidified as a distinct title — blockchain is increasingly added as a **specialization** to Backend Engineer, Infrastructure Engineer, or Solutions Architect.

**Recommendation:** Use **"Blockchain Engineer"** — sounds senior, reflects systems-level work (Go/Rust, protocols, distributed systems).

## 3. "AI Engineer" vs "AI Developer"

**AI Engineer is the clear winner.**

- LinkedIn "Jobs on the Rise" 2025 and 2026: **AI Engineer = #1 fastest-growing role** in the US
- "AI Developer" is virtually unused at serious companies
- Replacing "AI Software Developer" with "Machine Learning Engineer" yields a **2x increase** in qualified applicants
- AI Engineer > AI Developer > "AI Ninja" (the last one is a red flag for recruiters)

**AI Engineer market (2025-2026):**
- Key skills: LangChain, RAG, PyTorch, prompt engineering
- Hotspots: San Francisco, NYC, Dallas
- Industries: technology, IT services, business consulting
- The global economy added 1.3M+ roles (AI Engineers, Forward-Deployed Engineers, Data Annotators) over 2 years

**Recommendation:** Use **"AI Engineer"** — this is THE title recruiters search for.

## 4. "Software Engineer" vs "Senior Software Engineer" — Is It Worth Spending a Slot on Senior?

**Key finding: Seniority = a separate filter in LinkedIn Recruiter.**

LinkedIn Recruiter has **7 seniority levels** as a separate filter:
- Internship, Entry Level, Associate, Mid-Senior Level, Director, Executive, Not applicable

This means:
- A recruiter can search for "Software Engineer" + filter "Mid-Senior Level" — and get senior engineers
- LinkedIn **infers seniority** from title, company size, and other signals
- "Senior" in the title is NOT required to appear in the Senior filter

**But there's a nuance:**
- Many recruiters search **directly** by title "Senior Software Engineer" or "Senior Backend Engineer"
- Especially external recruiters (not corporate) — they often don't use advanced filters
- "Senior" in the title reinforces the visual signal and shows the level

**Recommendation:** Use **"Senior Backend Engineer"** (not just "Backend Engineer") — "Senior" doesn't waste a separate slot, it strengthens the main title. You DON'T need to add "Backend Engineer" separately without Senior — that wastes a slot.

## 5. Top 5 Job Titles for Open to Work (profile: Go, Rust, AI agents, blockchain, distributed systems, 10+ years)

LinkedIn allows a **maximum of 5 titles** in Open to Work. Titles are selected from LinkedIn's suggested list.

### Recommended Combination:

| # | Title | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Senior Backend Engineer** | Core, most searchable title. "Software Engineer" = #1 most in-demand job on LinkedIn |
| 2 | **AI Engineer** | #1 fastest-growing role 2025-2026. Covers AI agents, LLM, RAG |
| 3 | **Blockchain Engineer** | Covers the crypto/web3 market. Rust + Go = hard-to-fill, premium rates |
| 4 | **Senior Software Engineer** | Broad net for recruiters looking for general senior engineers |
| 5 | **Platform Engineer** | Covers distributed systems, infrastructure. Or **Distributed Systems Engineer** if available in LinkedIn's list |

### Alternative Options (if a title isn't in LinkedIn's dropdown):
- **Staff Engineer** — if targeting $200k+ roles
- **Infrastructure Engineer** — covers DevOps-adjacent roles
- **Solutions Architect** — if interested in pre-sales/consulting roles in blockchain

### What NOT to Put:
- ~~"Back-End Developer"~~ — outdated format
- ~~"AI Developer"~~ — doesn't exist as a recognized title
- ~~"Blockchain Developer"~~ — sounds junior
- ~~"Full Stack Engineer"~~ — not your profile, dilutes positioning
- ~~"DevOps Engineer"~~ — different specialization, distracts from backend/AI focus

### Specific Roles on the Market Right Now:

**Serotonin (Web3):**
- Senior Distributed Systems Engineer - Golang
- Senior Infrastructure Engineer
- Senior Data Engineer

**Zscaler:**
- Staff Site Reliability Engineer
- Rust Engineer
- Crypto Production Engineer

**OleOle (Web3):**
- Senior Rust Backend Engineer

**Jump Crypto / LayerZero Labs:**
- Backend Engineer (Go/Rust)
- Protocol Engineer

**Typical wording in postings:**
- "Senior Distributed Systems Engineer" — design, implementation, optimization of large-scale distributed infrastructure
- "Rust Infrastructure Engineer" — scalable APIs, data pipelines, high-performance backend
- "Protocol Engineer" — Rust/Substrate L1, custom pallets, ZK primitives
- "Backend Engineer (Go)" — blockchain/DeFi backend services

### Patterns:
1. Web3 companies more often use **"Engineer"** than **"Developer"**
2. Go and Rust are **hard-to-fill** roles, 450:1 applicant ratio but most lack production experience
3. "Distributed Systems" often appears in the title or requirements, not as a standalone role
4. AI + Blockchain intersection = **highest-paying roles** ("Agentic AI" on-chain)

## Additional Profile Recommendations

### Headline Format:
```
Senior Backend Engineer | AI & Blockchain | Go · Rust · Distributed Systems
```
Formula: `[Specific Role] | [Key Skill/Achievement] | [Industry Context]`

### Keywords to Spread Across the Profile:
- backend, distributed systems, microservices
- Go, Golang, Rust
- AI agents, LLM, RAG, LangChain
- blockchain, Web3, DeFi, smart contracts
- high-performance, scalable, fault-tolerant

### Open to Work Settings:
- Use **"Recruiters Only"** mode (not the green frame) — 3x response rate
- The green frame gives +40% InMails, but signals desperation to some hiring managers
- 70% of jobs are never posted — recruiter-only OTW covers this hidden market
