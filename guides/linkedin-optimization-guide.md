## 1. Headline

### Final Formula

```
Senior Go & Rust Engineer | AI Agents · Blockchain | Distributed Systems
```

73 characters. First 40 characters (visible on mobile): `Senior Go & Rust Engineer | AI Agents`

### Headline Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **First 40-60 characters are critical** | Mobile truncates at ~40, LinkedIn algorithm prioritizes the first 60 |
| **3-5 key phrases** | More = keyword stuffing, fewer = missed searches |
| **Separators: `\|` between blocks, `·` within** | Industry standard, best readability |
| **Front-load job title** | Recruiters scan left to right |
| **Maximum 220 characters** | Expanded from 120 in 2023-2024 |

### What to Include

| Element | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| **Senior** | ✅ Include | Recruiters filter by it, pre-qualifies for senior salary |
| **Stack (Go, Rust)** | ✅ Include | Rare combinations = strong signal. Rust = +15% salary premium |
| **AI Agents** | ✅ Include | #1 buzzword 2026, "AI Engineer" = +143% YoY title growth |
| **Blockchain** | ✅ Include | Top term for engineering roles |
| **Distributed Systems** | ✅ Include | Umbrella term = highload + microservices + scale |

### What NOT to Include

| Element | Why Not |
|---------|---------|
| **10+ Yrs / years of experience** | "Senior" already implies it, wastes characters. Years are visible in the profile |
| **Emoji** | No SEO value, signals junior, senior hiring managers dock points |
| **DeFi** | Only if you actually worked in it. Interviewers will ask "which protocols?" |
| **High-Load** | Russian-speaking term, not searched globally. Equivalent = "Distributed Systems" |
| **LLM Tooling / AI Tooling** | Outdated. "AI Agents" is more precise and hotter |
| **Crypto / Web3** | Generic, "Blockchain" is better for engineering roles |

### Headline for Experience Title

LinkedIn title = free-form field, you can write anything. For positions:
- `Team Lead | Senior Backend Engineer` — use pipe, not arrow
- `Founder & AI Engineer` — for current position
- `Senior Backend Engineer (Go)` — stack in parentheses

## 3. Open to Work — Job Titles

LinkedIn works with a **fixed vocabulary of titles** — it does not accept custom ones. Maximum 5.

### Recommended Five

| # | Title | What searches it catches |
|---|-------|------------------------|
| 1 | **Senior Software Engineer** | Broadest senior search |
| 2 | **AI Engineer** | AI-native companies, startups |
| 3 | **Back End Engineer** | Classic backend (Engineer > Developer) |
| 4 | **Blockchain Developer** | Crypto/Web3 companies |
| 5 | **Software Engineer** | Maximum broad coverage |

### How to Search for Titles in LinkedIn

- LinkedIn autocomplete is basic — type the first 3-4 letters and wait
- "Backend" (one word) might not be found — try "Back End" (two words)
- **Engineer > Developer** — more senior signal
- "Senior Backend Engineer" DOES NOT exist in LinkedIn's vocabulary — use "Back End Engineer"

### What NOT to Put in Titles

- ~~Full Stack Developer~~ — signals "can do everything" = nothing deeply
- ~~DevOps / SRE~~ — different profile
- ~~Team Lead / Engineering Manager~~ — narrows the funnel
- ~~Prompt Engineer~~ — commoditized

## 5. Location Strategy

### Why Dubai, Not Bali

| Factor | Dubai | Bali/Indonesia |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| **Recruiter search** | Recruiters search Dubai for MENA + global | "Indonesia" = 0 hits from US/EU recruiters |
| **Salary signal** | High expectations | Signals $500-2k (local market) |
| **Crypto hub** | Binance, OKX, Bybit HQ | No crypto reputation |
| **CompanyA connection** | CompanyA legal entity in Dubai | — |
| **EU timezone overlap** | UTC+4, 4-6 hours overlap | UTC+8, 1-2 hours overlap |

> "I'm remote, based in APAC with flexible hours. My previous company CompanyA had its main operating entity in Dubai. I have strong overlap with EMEA timezone."

### CompanyA Location Facts (for defense)

- **Company's legal entity in Dubai** (operating entity, in Terms of Service)
- **CompanyA Tech Ltd** — Hong Kong (LinkedIn HQ)
- Team: 400+ people, 27+ countries, fully remote

## 6. Summary (About)

### Structure

```
Hook (first 200 characters) → Bridge (career trajectory) → Proof (what I bring) → CTA
```

### Rules

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Maximum** | 2,600 characters |
| **Ideal** | 1,800-2,200 characters |
| **First 200-300 characters** | Visible without clicking "See more" — the most important part |
| **First person** | Always. Third person = press release |
| **Keywords** | Natural repetition 3-5 times across sections |
| **CTA** | Email + "Open to X roles" |
| **No em dash (—)** | Looks AI-generated. Use periods and commas |

### Hook Formula

```
I build [what] in [stack]. [X]+ years of [domain]. Currently [current activity].
```

### Career Trajectory Formula

```
Started with [Stack A] at [Company]. Moved to [Stack B] for [domain]. Switched to [Stack C] at [Company] ([achievement]). Now [current stack] for [current domain].

Each transition was driven by the next problem, not by trends.
```

### What I Bring — No Arrows or Dashes

List in blocks through paragraphs:
```
AI engineering: [specifics]
Go & Rust backend: [specifics]
Blockchain infrastructure: [specifics]
```

DO NOT use `→`, `—`, bullet points in Summary — LinkedIn renders them poorly and they look AI-generated.

## 7. Experience — How to Write

### Character limits

| Field | Limit | Visible without clicking |
|-------|-------|--------------------------|
| Experience title | 100 characters | Fully |
| Experience description | 2,000 characters | ~3 lines |

### Bullet Format

- **5-7 bullets per role** (up to 10 for current)
- **XYZ formula:** "Accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z"
- **First 2-3 bullets = most important** (recruiters scan)
- **Quantify:** RPS, MAU, uptime, team size, % improvement
- **Action verbs:** built, designed, scaled, reduced, led, optimized

### Honesty in Wording (4 levels)

| Level | Example | Verdict |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **1. Fabrication** | Inventing experience/stack you don't have | ❌ |
| **2. Embellishment** | "Led team of 10" (actually participated) | ❌ |
| **3. Strategic silence** | Not mentioning a gap | ✅ |
| **4. Strategic emphasis** | Highlighting strengths first | ✅ |

### Aurelius Test

> "Can you instantly, without pause, explain every line of your profile?"

If you need a pause to construct an explanation — rephrase it.

### "Led" vs "Contributed to" vs "Participated in"

- **Led** — you were responsible, made decisions, owned the outcome
- **Contributed to** — participated meaningfully but did not lead
- **Participated in** — were part of it, but others did most of the work

Choose based on honesty.

### Small Numbers — Don't Write Them

If metrics aren't impressive ($200K volume, 3K users) — use qualitative descriptions instead ("hundreds of top NFT collections", "low-latency token discovery"). Small numbers are worse than none.

### Multiple roles at one company

LinkedIn supports multiple positions under one company. Benefits:
- Each role = separate keyword surface
- Shows career progression
- Each gets its own Skills tags
- LinkedIn displays the total tenure

### CompanyA Split Example

```
CompanyA (Jul 2025 – Feb 2026)
├── Team Lead, Backend Engineering (Jul – Nov 2025)
└── Senior Backend Engineer (Nov 2025 – Feb 2026)
```

### Independent/Freelance periods

- Use "Independent" or "Self-employed" as the company name
- Title: "Software Engineer (Contract & Freelance)" or "Trading Systems Engineer"
- Describe contracts with "Contract:" prefix
- DO NOT use "Freelance" as a company name (LinkedIn has an employment type "Freelance")

### Gaps

Gaps of 2-6 months across a 10+ year career are normal — don't fill them with fake entries. If asked: "transition period", "freelance consulting", "crypto winter" — all valid.

## 8. Skills Strategy

### Quantity

- LinkedIn allows up to **100 skills**
- Only **top 3 (or 5) skills** are visible on the profile
- Recommendation: **30-50 relevant skills**, carefully choose the top ones

### Recommended Top 5

1. **Go (Programming Language)** — primary stack
2. **Distributed Systems** — senior signal
3. **AI Agents** — trending differentiator
4. **Rust** — premium skill
5. **Blockchain** — domain

### Hard Skills > Soft Skills

Recruiters filter by hard skills (Go, Kubernetes, Distributed Systems), not soft skills (Leadership, Communication). Soft skills go in descriptions, not in the Skills section.

### Endorsements

- **5+ endorsements per skill = 33x more recruiter contacts**
- Endorsed skills rank higher
- Endorsements from people in the same industry carry more weight
- Ask for endorsements on core skills (Go, Distributed Systems, AI Agents)

### Skill Assessments

- Top 30% = visible badge
- Free, adds credibility
- Available for Go, Python, REST APIs, Git, Linux, and more

### Skills Per Role

Attach skills to specific positions — recruiters see recency. 3-5 skills per role.

### Duplicates

LinkedIn has duplicates: "Go (Programming Language)" and "Golang (Go)". Keep both — different recruiters search differently.

## 9. LinkedIn Algorithm & SEO

### Ranking Factors (by weight)

| # | Factor | Weight |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | **Keyword match in headline + title** | Highest |
| 2 | **Profile completeness (All-Star)** | Very high (40x visibility) |
| 3 | **Network proximity** | High (1st > 2nd > 3rd connections) |
| 4 | **#OpenToWork** | High (LinkedIn confirms boost) |
| 5 | **Engagement / Activity** | Medium |
| 6 | **Skills match** | Medium |
| 7 | **InMail response rate** | Medium |

### Indexed Fields (confirmed)

1. **Headline** (highest priority)
2. **Job Title** in Experience
3. **About/Summary** (fully)
4. **Experience descriptions** (all text)
5. **Skills section**
6. **Recommendations text**
7. **Media descriptions and captions**
8. **Published content** (articles/posts)

### Keyword Placement Strategy

```
Headline (max weight)
  → About (first 3 lines)
    → Experience title
      → Experience bullets (first 2-3)
        → Skills section
```

Natural repetition of target keywords **3-5 times** across different sections. Not keyword stuffing — organic mentions.

### Current Position = 16x Visibility

Profiles without a current position get **16x fewer impressions** in recruiter search. Always have an active current role (YourCompany, Self-employed, anything).

## 11. Recruiter Behavior

### Typical Pattern

- **87%** of recruiters use LinkedIn
- **6-7 seconds** per profile view
- They look at: photo → headline → current position → experience (top 2-3 roles)
- **7** people get hired through LinkedIn every minute

### Conversion Funnel

```
~1000 profile views
  → ~25 recruiter messages (2.5%)
    → ~6 conversations
      → ~2-3 interviews
        → ~1 offer
```

Bottleneck = profile quality, not volume.

### LinkedIn Recruiter Filters

- Keywords (free text — searches across all fields)
- Location (city/country + radius)
- Workplace type (Remote / Hybrid / Onsite)
- Skills (standardized)
- Years of experience
- Current / Past company
- Open to Work status
- "Must have" / "Can have" / "Doesn't have" qualifiers

## 15. Premium & Open to Work Badge

| Feature | Cost | Result |
|---------|------|--------|
| **Open to Work (recruiter-only)** | Free | +40% InMail, no downsides |
| **Open to Work (public badge)** | Free | +40% InMail, but stigma for senior |
| **Premium Career** | $30/mo | 2.6x likelihood of being hired, "Top Applicant" badge = +43% response |

### Recruiter-only vs Public Badge

For senior ($150k+) roles: **Recruiter only**. The green frame:
- Removes the sense of exclusivity
- "Pick me" energy for senior = negative
- Hiring managers subconsciously think: "if they're that good, why is nobody hiring them?"

Recruiter-only = recruiters see you're Open to Work (special badge in LinkedIn Recruiter), but publicly the profile looks like an employed professional.

## 17. Content Strategy

### Posting

- **2-3 posts/week** — consistency matters more than volume
- **Best time:** Tue-Thu, 8-10 AM in the target audience's timezone
- **Posts > Articles** for visibility. Articles for credibility + Google SEO

### What Works

- Technical deep-dives (architecture decisions, scaling stories)
- "Here's what I learned building X" posts
- Open source project announcements
- Career milestone posts (tastefully done)

### What Does NOT Work

- Motivational quotes
- Engagement bait ("agree?" / "like if you think...")
- Reposted memes
- Generic AI-generated commentary

### Content Strategy for Job Search

1. Weekly technical post: "How I approach X in Go" (patterns, tools)
2. AI agents commentary: opinions on agent frameworks, orchestration patterns
3. Engagement: comment on posts by hiring managers at target companies
4. Share interesting Go/AI open source work (yours or others with commentary)

## 18. Honesty Framework

From the research `honesty-authenticity-self-presentation-2026-03.md`:

### Four Levels of Self-Presentation

| Level | Example | Verdict |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **1. Fabrication** | Inventing experience you don't have | ❌ Lying |
| **2. Embellishment** | "Led team of 10" (actually participated) | ❌ Violates satya |
| **3. Strategic silence** | Not mentioning a gap | ✅ Acceptable |
| **4. Strategic emphasis** | Highlighting strengths first | ✅ Mastery |

### Key Principles

- **Aurelius Test:** "Can you instantly explain every line?"
- **Buddha's Matrix:** Truth + usefulness = speak. Truth + uselessness = stay silent. Lying = never
- **Self-Verification Theory:** authentic candidates achieve better P-O fit and receive more offers (Cable & Kay study, Academy of Management Journal)
- **Fake profile = reverse filter:** attracts companies that value facade over substance

### Practical Boundaries

- ❌ Lying about stack (Go instead of Kotlin) — fabrication
- ❌ Inflating employment dates — fabrication
- ❌ Claiming team achievements as personal ("Led" when "Participated") — embellishment
- ✅ Not listing ContractCo Labs (3 months, irrelevant) — strategic silence
- ✅ Emphasizing AI engineering over maintenance — strategic emphasis
- ✅ "hundreds of top NFT collections" instead of "$200K volume" — qualitative framing

## 20. SSI (Social Selling Index)

**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/sales/ssi

### Current Score (2026-03-23): 33/100

| Component | Score | Max | Status |
|-----------|-------|-----|--------|
| Establish professional brand | 13.8 | 25 | ✅ Profile updated |
| Build relationships | 11.5 | 25 | ✅ 500+ connections |
| Find the right people | 7.5 | 25 | 🟡 Needed: targeted connections |
| **Engage with insights** | **0.2** | **25** | 🔴 Main gap |

### What SSI Affects

- Position in recruiter search (higher SSI = more frequent results)
- Feed visibility (posts and comments shown to more people)
- "People You May Know" recommendations
- LinkedIn data: SSI 70+ = 45% more opportunities

### Plan to Raise SSI to 60+

**Engage with insights (0.2 → 15+):** THE main lever
- 10-15 substantive comments per day (15+ words each)
- Comment on posts by CTOs and engineers from target companies
- At least 1 technical post per week
- LinkedIn penalizes generic/AI comments

**Find the right people (7.5 → 15+):**
- 5-10 targeted connection requests per day
- Hiring managers, engineers from Provable, Hibachi, Bloq, Synonym, etc.
- Personalized requests (+15% acceptance)

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks of active engagement = SSI 50-60
